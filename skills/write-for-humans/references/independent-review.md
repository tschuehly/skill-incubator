# Independent review

Use a fresh reader when a draft:

- is at least 800 words;
- adapts source material where fidelity matters; or
- informs a consequential legal, medical, financial, security, architecture, or operational
  decision.

Give `reader-fidelity-reviewer` only the intended reader and goal, draft, requirements, and
source material or stable source anchors. Keep the authoring conversation and reasoning out of
the review context.

Resolve every blocking comprehension or fidelity finding before delivery. If a correction
materially changes the affected passage, rerun the reviewer on that passage. The review is
complete when the reviewer reports no blocking issue and the author has completed the skill's
self-check.

When the runtime cannot start a fresh reviewer, report independent review as unavailable and
label the author's check as self-review.

The premise

Field technicians lose connectivity for 20–90 minutes in basements and plant rooms. Today the app blocks on every write, so a lost signal means the job sheet is filled in twice: once on paper, once in the evening.

Conflict policy

A merge that a technician cannot explain to a customer is worse than a refusal. The question is who decides when two edits disagree, and how visible that is.

job.status      = "complete"   (device A, 14:02, offline)
job.status      = "needs-parts" (device B, 14:05, offline)
job.notes      += "seal replaced" (device A, 14:03, offline)

Rollout

Offline writes cannot be dark-launched: the technician sees a different app the moment the queue exists. Whatever we ship first is what a region's technicians learn.

What it costs to be wrong

Per-field merge is roughly three weeks and reversible. A CRDT document is roughly seven and effectively permanent — the stored shape stops being queryable, so reporting is rewritten with it. That asymmetry is why this round exists.

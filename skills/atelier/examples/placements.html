Thread placements

Waiting on you: · waiting on the agent:

below

The default. The thread sits under the content, so a short Region keeps the comment and its context on screen together.

side

The thread moves into a gutter beside the content. Right when the content is narrow — a comparison, a short list, a table.

sheet

The thread opens in a panel fixed to the bottom of the viewport. The document keeps scrolling normally behind it, so an arbitrarily long Region can still be read while its thread stays visible.

Search index migration

Move search to the new index by 14 November

The new index answers the slowest 1% of queries in 90 ms instead of 1.4 s. Moving takes three steps over four weeks; only the third one changes what customers see, and it can be rolled back while the old index still receives writes.

Data flow during the move

  1. The API writes every document to the old and the new index.
  2. A shadow comparer sends 1% of reads to both and records every differing result.
  3. Reads switch to the new index once differences stay under 0.5% for seven days.

Rollout steps

1 · Dual write, from 20 October

Every write lands in both indexes. It costs about 18% more write latency at p95 for two weeks.

2 · Shadow read, from 27 October

1% of reads also query the new index. The comparer has logged 0.3% differing results in staging, all in ranking order, none in matches.

3 · Cutover, from 10 November

Reads switch region by region, Europe first. Rollback is a flag flip while dual write is still on.

Risks

  • Ranking changes reach customers even when every document matches: 0.3% of staging queries reorder their top five.

  • Dual write doubles indexing cost for two weeks, about €1,900.

  • The comparer samples 1% of reads, so a query that runs a few times a day may never be compared.

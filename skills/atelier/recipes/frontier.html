Offline-first sync for field technicians

Offline sync ends 6.4 hours of duplicate entry per technician a week. Every sync model except last-write-wins merges edits a technician may not be able to explain to a customer, and the rollout depends on who resolves two disagreeing edits.

Is the dropout figure the right basis?

Plan §1: "38% of sessions lose connectivity; offline writes remove the paper fallback."

Telemetry 1–30 June, 214 sessions: 38% had a dropout, the median dropout lasted 31 minutes, the 95th percentile 88 minutes. The June firmware update changed the radio driver on half the fleet.

What ships to the first region?

Plan §4: "Ship offline writes to all regions in the 3.0 release."

Offline writes cannot be dark-launched: technicians see a different app the moment the write queue exists, and whatever ships first is what a region's technicians learn.

Is seven weeks acceptable to remove the reporting risk?

Plan §5: "Budget: three weeks of client and server work."

Per-field merge takes about three weeks and can be reverted. A CRDT document takes about seven and cannot: the stored shape stops being queryable, so reporting is rewritten with it.

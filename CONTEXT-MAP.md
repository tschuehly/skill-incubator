# Context Map

This incubator contains independent skill domains. Each skill owns its language; terms are
shared only when a relationship is listed here.

## Contexts

- [Atelier](./skills/atelier/CONTEXT.md) — creates and operates task-shaped interaction surfaces
- [Pi Workbench](../pi-workbench/docs/foundation/vocabulary.md) — allocates human and model attention across Workstreams and project Runs

## Relationships

- Atelier is self-contained. Other skills may use an Atelier review surface without adopting its
  interaction details.
- Pi Workbench may present a Surface through Atelier. Pi Workbench owns the Run, authority,
  evidence, and required human judgments; Atelier owns task-shaped presentation and feedback capture.
